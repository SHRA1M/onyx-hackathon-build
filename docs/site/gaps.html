UAE position

The human requirement is binding (26.7.2, licence 9.1) and Mizan enforces it. The CPR wants first-contact resolution (26.14.1) and bars the machine from the conversation (26.7.2); nothing joins the two: no text says what an automated channel must do at the moment a conversation becomes a complaint, or how fast the human must take over. Two duties meet at that moment: licence 9.1 has complaints answered «دون إبطاء» (without delay; the English text reads "promptly"; the Arabic binds, licence 16.7), and Trade Law 14/2023 Art 6(7) has complaint means "always available without interruption with qualified staff"; neither sets a hand-over or a time. The acknowledgement clock (26.13.3) names telephone, visit and written complaints, not chat or app, and a reference number is not a conversation.

Recommendation
