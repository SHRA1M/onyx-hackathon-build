Proposed rule, not law

proposed-gap-1, proposed, not law, for TDRA.

When a conversation on an automated channel becomes a Consumer Complaint (CPR 2.1.7), the non-human agent stops and a named trained human (licence 9.1) takes the conversation within two minutes of the trigger; the hand-over moment is recorded and is the start of the T1(2a) measurement.
