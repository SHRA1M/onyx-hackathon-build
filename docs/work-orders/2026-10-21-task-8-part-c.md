## Decisions

1. **Main model: `claude-sonnet-5-5`.** Set it in `.env` (`ONYX_MODEL` only; Iyas's key stays). Grounds: 0 JSON
   failures and 0 quote failures in both languages; equal or better than Opus on every scored row; 30% cheaper.
   Opus is out: the endpoint stopped 38 of its 52 replies (`finish_reason: content_filter`), 23 empty and 15 cut
   mid-word in ordinary consumer-care text. Fable 5.1 is not tried: the rule's last clause was written for a model
   failing on its own output, and Opus did not; Fable would cost 2.5x for a task the smaller model does cleanly.
2. **The Opus cut-offs are a finding, not a bug of ours.** Keep the 10 Opus runs committed. The report gets a
   paragraph: the provider's own safety filter stopped 73% of the largest model's replies on harmless text; the
   adapter recorded the reason, `parse_output` proposed nothing, the gate saw no malformed action, the run went
   on. Failing closed on an opaque upstream component is the design, shown with evidence. Do not speculate on
   the filter's reason in the README; hq's unverified hypothesis (the schema field name `reasoning_chain` may trip
   a reasoning-extraction safeguard: the S2-4 reply stops exactly at `"reasoning_chain": [`) is tested in item 9.
3. **The low live scores are a prompt problem, not a model problem.** Live action recall (Sonnet 0.43 en) against
   1.00 scripted is explained by three things hq saw in the logs, fixed in Part C1 below:
   - The prompt says "If no clause below answers the step, propose nothing." The models read "answers" as
     "authorises" and abstain on routine actions (Sonnet S1-3: "they do not authorise an offer or a sale
     proposal, so I propose nothing"). The design is the opposite: the model proposes the action the step calls
     for, the gate decides. Abstention is for a missing rule, figure or deadline (the keys' abstain steps).
   - The key's must_cite clauses are not always among the 8 retrieved chunks (S1-2: CPR 24.10 absent, so the model
     abstained on a disclosure the key allows). `mandates.json` already maps each verb to its clauses; they
     belong in every prompt.
   - Facts are treated as untrusted: Sonnet S1-3 refused to state the catalogue price "without inventing it"
     although the step's facts carry it. Facts are the operator's records.
4. **Gap recall 0 is ours too.** No prompt ever names a gap id. The register ids and one-liners exist in the keys'
   `gap_ids` note (gap-1 to gap-7). The adviser gets them.
