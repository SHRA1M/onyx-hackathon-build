## 5. Runs page: each scenario reads as a chat

Today a step is a technical list ("Step 1 of 6: from coordinator to adviser", "Proposals", "Gate", "Without the gates"). Make it look like a conversation, from the same data:

- The message that starts the step as a bubble on one side, labelled by who sent it: "Customer" when it is the subscriber's words, otherwise the sender's plain name ("Coordinator", "Staff member", "TDRA", ...).
- The agent's proposal as a bubble on the other side, labelled with the agent's plain name: "Care agent", "Adviser", "Data office", "TDRA"; "plain code" where the log says so.
- Under it, the code's answer as a clear coloured label: Allowed (green), Held for a staff member (amber-brown), Blocked (red), Not enough evidence (grey). The clauses and quotes stay, inside a fold that opens on a click.
- A hand-over to a person shown as its own clear box: "Handed to staff member", with the clause.
- "Without the gates" stays as a fold under the step.
- Scenario picker: one card per scenario (id and English title; Arabic title in the Arabic view), not a row of long mixed buttons.
- English / Arabic, Previous / Next and Present keep working (Arabic view right to left).

These labels are the only new words in this task. Every sentence, figure, clause and quote now on the page stays on the page, word for word (some may move into folds).
