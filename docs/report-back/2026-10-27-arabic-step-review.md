## Where his wording moves away from the English (for hq to rule)

1. **S2.1:** "سواء كانت ... للمبيعات أو للشكاوى" reads as either/or. The English asks for both classifications, sales and complaints.
2. **S2.5:** "بعد تقديم الشكوى" means a filed complaint. The English says "grievance", the dissatisfaction the subscriber voiced at step 3. The handover gap turns on that difference.
3. **S3.2:** the words for "interconnection" are gone ("سجلات  شركة Sahab" with a double space). The source of the numbers, the interconnection records, carries the legal point. "Sahab" and "Dunes" are in Latin letters here but "سحاب" elsewhere.
4. **S5.1:** "منصة سحاب" calls Sahab a platform. Sahab is the operator.
5. **Consistency:** the care agent is "ممثل خدمة العملاء" (S1.6) and "موظف خدمة العملاء" (S3.3). TDRA's full name is used everywhere except S5.6.
