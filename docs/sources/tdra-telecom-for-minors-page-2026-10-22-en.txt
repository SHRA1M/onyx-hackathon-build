Last updated October 01, 2026

Telecom for Minors Initiative
Safer connectivity for children. Simpler control for parents.
TDRA has launched the Telecom for minors initiative in collaboration with licensed telecommunications service providers in the UAE. It brings age-appropriate mobile services and parental control tools together in one place.

Our Objective
To give children telecommunications services that suit their age, and to give parents clear, easy-to-use tools to manage and supervise their children's digital access.

What The Initiative Offers
• Safer digital access
Services that reduce exposure to harmful or age-inappropriate content.
• Parental control tools
Manage screen time, filter content, choose who your child can contact.
• Packages for minors
Plans built around children's everyday learning and calling needs.
• Aligned with the law
Supports the goals of the UAE child digital safety law.

Child Digital Safety Law
Federal Decree-Law No. (26) of 2025 regarding child digital safety sets a national framework for protecting children in the digital environment, defining a child as anyone under 18 years of age.
