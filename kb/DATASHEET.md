## How documents were fetched and verified

`python -m onyx kb fetch` downloads each VERIFIED document from its manifest `url` to `kb/sources/`, keeps it only when its sha256 equals the manifest's, and otherwise copies the maintainer's local file when that file's sha256 matches; it records `url_accessed` and `http_status`. `python -m onyx kb verify` checks every fetched source against its sha256 and every URL against the listing-page and landing-page rules. Where a text layer cannot be read, a labelled transcription sits in `kb/extracts/`: `decision-16-2026-ar.txt`, `licence-2-2026-ar.txt`.
